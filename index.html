🔎 Logic for Adjusting Kirana Service Areas

Each Kirana is given an initial circular service area (depending on mode). After entering performance inputs, the score for each Kirana is calculated using the weighted formula:

Score = (0.30 × OTD) + (0.25 × FADR) + (20 × (CFS / 5)) − (0.15 × RR) − (0.10 × CR)

  • OTD: On-Time Delivery (%)
  • FADR: First Attempt Delivery Rate (%)
  • CFS: Customer Feedback Score (1–5)
  • RR: Return Rate (%)
  • CR: Complaint Rate (%)

Then, the Neutral Score is computed as the average score of all Kiranas. Each Kirana’s final radius is adjusted relative to this Neutral Score:

Final Radius = Base Radius + (Score − Neutral Score) × Factor

  • Base Radius: 450 m (Sub-Urban) or 310 m (Dense Urban)
  • Factor: 5 (i.e., every 1 point above/below neutral = ±5 m change)
  • For Dense-Urban areas , the max and min radii are 500 m and 400m .
  • For Sub-Urban areas , the max and min radii are 360 m and 260m .

This ensures that high-performing Kiranas cover a larger area, while lower-scoring Kiranas have their coverage reduced, all within controlled limits.
